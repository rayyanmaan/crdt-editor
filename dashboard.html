CRDT replicas

Each panel is a separate replica process with its own copy of the state. Edits apply locally at once. Text edits are sent to the others as ops, and anything lost is re-sent by the version-vector repair every second; the counter and set travel in a full-state sync. Kill a replica to crash it (SIGKILL); restart it and it comes back empty with a new incarnation and catches up from its peers.
